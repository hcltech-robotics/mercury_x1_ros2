# HCLTech facts as of 4 November 2026 (latest results: Q2 FY27, quarter ended 30 September 2026)

HCLTech's financial year runs from April to March, so FY27 is April 2026 to March 2027 and Q2 FY27 is July to September 2026.
These facts replace the older Q3 FY25 numbers in hcltech_q3fy25.md wherever they overlap.
Facts marked "(reported)" come from news coverage and were not confirmed in an HCLTech or stock exchange document.

## Results Q2 FY27 (July to September 2026)

HCLTech announced its Q2 FY27 results on 12 October 2026.

HCLTech revenue in Q2 FY27 was 3.724 billion US dollars, up 2.0 percent quarter on quarter and up 2.2 percent year on year in dollar terms.
HCLTech revenue in Q2 FY27 was 35,620 crore rupees, up 3.0 percent quarter on quarter and up 11.5 percent year on year.
In constant currency, HCLTech revenue grew 2.3 percent quarter on quarter and 2.6 percent year on year in Q2 FY27.
HCLTech services revenue in Q2 FY27 was 3.442 billion dollars, up 3.0 percent quarter on quarter and 4.0 percent year on year in constant currency.

HCLTech's EBIT margin in Q2 FY27 was 18.2 percent, up 1.31 percentage points from the previous quarter.
HCLTech's operating profit, or EBIT, in Q2 FY27 was 677 million dollars, or 6,465 crore rupees.
Excluding restructuring costs of 0.84 percentage points, HCLTech's EBIT margin in Q2 FY27 was 19.0 percent.

HCLTech net income in Q2 FY27 was 5,132 crore rupees, its highest quarterly net income ever, up 11.0 percent quarter on quarter and up 21.2 percent year on year.
HCLTech net income in Q2 FY27 was 538 million dollars, which is 14.4 percent of revenue.
HCLTech's diluted earnings per share for the twelve months to September 2026 were 70.22 rupees.

HCLTech's new deal bookings in Q2 FY27 were 3.258 billion dollars, up 27 percent year on year and 35 percent quarter on quarter.
Q2 FY27 was HCLTech's second-highest bookings quarter ever, and the first half of FY27 was its strongest first half for bookings.

HCLTech's Advanced AI revenue in Q2 FY27 was 219 million dollars, up 28.4 percent quarter on quarter and 78 percent year on year in constant currency.
HCLTech's annualized Advanced AI revenue reached 876 million dollars in Q2 FY27.
HCLTech said its agentic AI offerings passed 100 million dollars of revenue per quarter in Q2 FY27.
HCLTech said its AI Factory business grew 64 percent quarter on quarter in Q2 FY27.

HCLTech had 230,339 employees on 30 September 2026, after adding 6,450 people in Q2 FY27.
HCLTech hired 6,136 freshers in Q2 FY27.
HCLTech's attrition was 12.8 percent for the twelve months to September 2026.
Women made up 30.0 percent of HCLTech's workforce on 30 September 2026.
HCLTech's revenue per employee was 65,400 dollars per year as of Q2 FY27.

HCLTech declared an interim dividend of 12 rupees per share for Q2 FY27, with record date 16 October 2026 and payment date 26 October 2026.
HCLTech paid out 60 rupees per share in dividends over the twelve months to September 2026, about 88.6 percent of its net income.

HCLTech's twelve-month revenue to September 2026 was 14.8 billion dollars.
HCLTech had gross cash of 2.66 billion dollars and net cash of 2.56 billion dollars on 30 September 2026.
HCLTech's return on invested capital was 41.2 percent for the twelve months to September 2026.

## Q2 FY27 by segment, industry and region

IT and Business Services was 75.4 percent of HCLTech revenue in Q2 FY27 and grew 4.4 percent year on year in constant currency.
Engineering and R&D Services was 16.7 percent of HCLTech revenue in Q2 FY27 and grew 2.1 percent year on year in constant currency.
HCLSoftware was 7.9 percent of HCLTech revenue in Q2 FY27, with revenue of 294 million dollars, down 11.2 percent year on year in constant currency.
HCLTech said the HCLSoftware revenue drop in Q2 FY27 came mainly from fewer multi-year licence renewals falling in that quarter.
HCLSoftware's annual recurring revenue was 1.134 billion dollars in Q2 FY27, up 7.8 percent year on year, or 2.6 percent without the Jaspersoft acquisition.

Financial Services was HCLTech's largest industry in Q2 FY27, at 22.4 percent of services revenue, growing 7.1 percent year on year.
Retail and Consumer Packaged Goods was HCLTech's fastest-growing industry in Q2 FY27, up 14.0 percent year on year.
Manufacturing was 18.3 percent of HCLTech services revenue in Q2 FY27, and Technology and Services was 14.3 percent.

The USA was 56.1 percent of HCLTech services revenue in Q2 FY27 and grew 3.5 percent year on year.
Europe was 26.9 percent of HCLTech services revenue in Q2 FY27 and fell 0.3 percent year on year.
India was HCLTech's fastest-growing region in Q2 FY27, up 21.8 percent year on year.

Acquisitions added about 35.6 million dollars of HCLTech revenue in Q2 FY27: about 21 million dollars from HPE's Telco Solutions business and about 15 million dollars from Jaspersoft.

## Company basics

HCL was founded in 1976 as one of India's original technology, computing and engineering startups.
HCLTech has been publicly listed since 2000, on the National Stock Exchange of India as HCLTECH and on the BSE as scrip code 532281.
HCLTech's headquarters are in Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India, and its registered office is in New Delhi.
The HCL Group celebrates its 50th anniversary in 2026.
HCLTech has more than 230,000 employees in 60 countries, from 167 nationalities, as of late 2026.
HCLTech has 205 global delivery centers and more than 70 labs.
More than 18,000 clients use HCLTech's software and products, and HCLTech holds more than 2,200 patents.

HCLTech has three business segments: IT and Business Services, Engineering and R&D Services, and HCLSoftware.
HCLTech serves Financial Services, Manufacturing, Life Sciences and Healthcare, Technology and Services, Semiconductor, Telecom and Media, Retail and Consumer Goods, Mobility, and Public Services.

## AI strategy, platforms and brands

HCLTech's stated ambition is to become the leading AI solutions company for global enterprises.
HCLTech's AI strategy has five pillars: transforming its own services with AI, building AI products, adding new AI services, scaling partnerships, and training AI talent.
HCLTech AI Force is HCLTech's AI platform for software engineering, modernization, data engineering, business operations and IT operations.
HCLTech AI Force moved to version 2.1 during FY26 and was used in more than 75 client accounts, and a newer version came out in Q2 FY27.
The HCLTech AI Force Agent Store offers more than 900 AI agents as of October 2026.
HCLTech has built more than 20 industry-specific AI solutions as of October 2026.
HCLTech's AI Factory offering designs, builds and runs AI data centers, and its AI Labs ran more than 400 client engagements in FY26.
HCLTech's Physical AI offerings include VisionX for AI visual inspection, Kinetic AI for robotics, TraceX for asset tracking, IEdgeX for edge AI, and SmartTwin for digital twins.
VisionX 3.0, released in Q2 FY27, added a fully agentic design and new versions for the oil and gas and mobility industries.
LUMI is HCLTech's internal agentic AI platform for employee help and questions.
HCLTech Pulse is a business unit launched in 2026 for fast-growing companies with annual revenue of 500 million to 5 billion dollars.
HCLSoftware products include HCL BigFix, HCL AppScan, HCL Unica, HCL Commerce, HCL DX, HCL UnO, HCL DevOps Loop, Informix, and the Actian data products such as Ingres and Zeenea.
HCL DevOps Loop was named a Visionary in the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for DevSecOps Platforms.

## Robotics and Physical AI at HCLTech

On 17 November 2025, HCLTech and NVIDIA opened a Physical AI Innovation Lab in Santa Clara, California, for physical AI and cognitive robotics.
The Santa Clara lab combines NVIDIA Omniverse, Metropolis, Isaac Sim, Jetson and Holoscan with HCLTech's VisionX, Kinetic AI, IEdgeX and SmartTwin solutions.
The Santa Clara lab is part of HCLTech's global AI Lab network and helps large companies test and scale physical AI.
HCLTech said in November 2025 that its physical AI clients include a leading port company, the world's leading high-tech company and a European mining company.
Kinetic AI.DCOps is HCLTech's data center operations solution that uses AI-powered robots with teleoperated, human-in-the-loop oversight.
Kinetic AI.DCOps robots handle tasks such as server reboots, fault detection and hot-swap drive replacement, and use vision-language-action models to understand instructions.
Kinetic AI.DCOps uses NVIDIA Omniverse simulation to train the AI and the hardware before deployment.
In Q1 FY27, a European manufacturer chose HCLTech to build the navigation, motion planning and system integration for its next-generation autonomous robot.
In Q1 FY27, a global mining company chose HCLTech's Physical AI solution, a single-robot system for industrial inspection and safety monitoring.
HCLTech said in October 2026 that its Physical AI revenue keeps growing across robotics, IoT and edge AI.
HCLTech was named a Market Shaper in the 2026 Gartner Emerging Market Quadrant for Physical AI Services, and was the only India-headquartered systems integrator in that quadrant.
HCLTech was named a Leader in the 2026 ISG Provider Lens for Intelligent Robotics and Physical AI Services.
HCLTech and SAP are working together on AI for physical and industrial settings such as warehouse operations, fleet management and 3D reality capture.
